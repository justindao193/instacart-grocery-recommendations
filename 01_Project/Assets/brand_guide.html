Using the Instacart brand

The look follows Instacart's public site and brand guide so the concept feels at home in their app, without claiming to be theirs.

Do

  • Use the palette and colour rules on this page
  • Use an official logo file only if you've obtained it legitimately, unmodified, with the disclaimer nearby
  • Use licensed stock photos
  • Name the data: "Instacart's public 2017 order dataset"
  • Show the disclaimer on every screen and case-study page

Don't

  • Say or imply this is Instacart's work
  • Redraw, recolour or stretch the logo or carrot
  • Copy Instacart's product or marketing photos
  • Present offline scores as Instacart results or business impact

Logo

No logo file is in the project (the brand guide's downloads need a staff login). The prototype header shows 03_Code/05_Prototype/assets/instacart_logo.svg or .png if you add one, and plain text "instacart" until then.

Rules

  • Carrot and Lime are spot colours: logo only, never backgrounds or body text.
  • Primary actions are green, never neutral.
  • No new tints, shades or off-brand colours.
  • The near-black reviewer bar (#1D1E21) is the one dark surface, because it's a demo tool, not the store.

Voice and copy

The store sounds like a calm grocery app. The method gets explained on Compare methods and About, not on cards.

Portfolio writing: first person, casual, contractions, honest about AI help, clear about measured vs assumed. Say "percentage points" for differences between percentages.

Imagery

  • Unsplash stock photos showing the kind of product, not an exact branded item. Credit "Photos from Unsplash".
  • Embedded in the prototype so it works offline and in previews.
  • No Instacart product photos, no faces, avoid other brands' packaging.
  • If a photo fails, show the soft bag placeholder, never a broken image.
  • No invented prices, sizes, stock, delivery or checkout anywhere in visuals.

Using it in the portfolio

  • Screenshot at 1366px (desktop) and 390px (phone). Show one "✓ Added", a filled basket and a Hide → Undo moment.
  • Keep the short disclaimer visible in at least one screenshot; put the full one in the case study.
  • Caption what's real (product names, method, results) and what's sample (example shopper histories).
  • Credit: "Built with AI assistance (Codex for analysis and code, Claude for the prototype design)."
  • Use these colour tokens for charts so the case study matches the prototype.
